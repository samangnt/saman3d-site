03 / THE ARTIST BEHIND THE PIXELS

Hi, I’m Saman.
I turn “what if” into “there it is”.

5

years of creating
in three dimensions

From a children’s film to a frozen city, I love finding the character in every idea. I work across modelling, rigging, animation, lighting and rendering, with Blender at the heart of my process.

I’ve created educational films for Dr. Gaila, animated a dental awareness episode for Songbird Media, built stream visuals for Pahadi Gaming, and brought short-form stories to life for creators around the world.

Alongside animation, I’m exploring game development and using scripting and AI-assisted tools to simplify repetitive production work—leaving more room for the creative part.
